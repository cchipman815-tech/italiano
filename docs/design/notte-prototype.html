    The motion system

    Four durations and four curves, all from the motion-design skill's Material 3 values. Nothing uses linear, and exits are always shorter than entrances: something leaving shouldn't hold your attention. Every duration scales with one variable, which is how Slow ×5 works.

    DurationValueScaleUsed for
    micro150msPress, toggle knob, chips
    small250msFades, tab pill, exits, dim
    medium400msSheet up, screen push, reflow
    large550msCard flip, session complete
    stagger40ms / itemSheet rows, first entrance only
    CurveValueUsed for
    standard0.2, 0, 0, 1Moving between on-screen positions
    decel0, 0, 0, 1Entering: sheets, pushed screens, the next card
    accel0.3, 0, 1, 1Leaving: dismissed sheets, flung cards
    sharp0.4, 0, 0.2, 1Temporary exits that come back: the screen underneath

    What doesn't move

    • Nothing loops on its own. The old 24-second glow drift is gone.
    • Lists don't replay their entrance every time you visit.
    • No parallax, no count-ups on load, no confetti.
    • Never more than 3 flashes a second, and every moving state also leaves a color, icon or label behind.

    Organization and color

    Four paths and 19 topics hold all 208 cards. Each card keeps its id and its Prego chapter tag, so your progress carries over and the chapter filter keeps working.

    Organization

    • 4 paths → 19 topics, grouped by how you learn them
    • Parole: nouns by theme, always with article and gender
    • Verbi: by conjugation pattern, idioms with their verb
    • Descrivere: adjectives with all four forms
    • Frasi: phrases by situation
    • Prego chapter: a filter and a small tag, not a folder

    Next step

    Every screen now has a design. When it feels right, the build starts on its own branch: tokens first, then the tab shell, then each screen.

    Decided

    • Abbina (match) stays, linked from the topic sheet for Parole, Verbi and Descrivere
    • Coniugazioni saves progress like every other mode, so known forms count down on Oggi
    • Saved translations are individual: Chance and Jennifer each have their own list (switch user to see it)
    • Loading, error and not-found screens get built from the States section

    New in the design

    • Undo instead of “are you sure?” for saved rows and cards
    • Aggiungi a un argomento: turn a saved translation into a card (a small new API route)
    • New topics are created inside a path, with an optional Prego chapter
    • Two-step delete for cards and topics, instead of the browser’s pop-up confirm

    Still open

    • Anything to change in the organization map
    • Whether Giorno looks right, and the default Italian level
    • The pace of the motion, the arc on the card fling, and whether the glow should breathe with pronunciation
